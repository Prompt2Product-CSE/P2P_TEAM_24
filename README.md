# About the Challenge


About the Challenge

Prompt2Product is a product-building challenge where participants are given a problem statement and must turn it into a suitable digital product within the given time.

The challenge focuses on:

*Understanding the problem
*Identifying user needs
*Product ideation
*UI/UX design
*User flow
*Creativity
*Ability to adapt to a surprise requirement

---

# Challenge Format

## Round 1 – Idea & Design

The problem statement will be revealed at the beginning of the challenge.

Participants must:

1. Understand the problem.
2. Brainstorm a creative solution.
3. Plan their product approach.
4. Design the solution architecture.
5. Present a clear and relevant product idea.

**Duration: 45 Minutes**

Only the best-performing teams will be shortlisted for Round 2 based on problem understanding, creativity, and proposed solution design.

---

## Round 2 – Build & Code

Shortlisted teams must develop a **functional MVP** based on their proposed solution.

Participants must:

1. Develop the functional MVP.
2. Implement the proposed features.
3. Use suitable development, programming and AI tools.
4. Complete the MVP within the allotted time.
5. Prepare the MVP for final demonstration.

**Duration: 1 Hour 45 Minutes**

---

## Final Evaluation – MVP Showcase

At the end of Round 2, teams must demonstrate their completed MVP to the judges and explain:

* Their solution
* Key features
* Functionality
* Use of AI/tools
* Real-world impact

---

## Rules & Regulations

1. All work must be completed within the allotted time.
2. Only shortlisted teams from Round 1 can proceed to Round 2.
3. Participants must be ready to demonstrate their MVP.
4. Personal laptops are mandatory.
5. Computer lab facilities will be provided for Round 2 participants.
6. Suitable development and AI tools may be used.
7. Any violation of the event rules may result in disqualification.
8. Judges' and organizing committee's decisions will be final.
